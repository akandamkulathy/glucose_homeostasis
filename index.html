About

EnergyFlow

An interactive model of human energy metabolism. Each box is a reservoir of chemical energy and each arrow is a rate of transfer between reservoirs; insulin and glucagon regulate those rates.

Why it is built this way

A conventional simulation provides controls and graphical output but leaves the intervening mechanism hidden, while a static diagram shows the mechanism but cannot be run. This model presents both together, and the guided modules extend the same system to clinical reasoning.

Scope

A teaching model of energy flow rather than a clinical tool. Its parameters were fitted to reproduce established physiological behavior rather than derived from first principles, so it is suitable for studying direction, sequence, and approximate magnitude.
